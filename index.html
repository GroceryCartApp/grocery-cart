FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about GroceryCart.

Can I type instead of using the microphone?+

Yes. GroceryCart is voice-first, but you can also type manually whenever you prefer.

Can I build a list while reading recipes?+

Yes. You can browse recipe websites and add ingredients to your list while you read.

Can I get recipe ideas from leftovers?+

Yes. Enter what you already have and GroceryCart helps you find recipe ideas.

Do I need an account?+

No. GroceryCart does not require an account for the main features.

Why are screenshots in English?+

They are examples. The app localizes interface text and item names in the selected language.
